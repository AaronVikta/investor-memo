Signature Documentation

Everything Signature has built, laid out in the open.

A grounded account of what the platform does, what has actually been verified, and what is still in progress. It covers the business case, the technical proof of concept, the underlying math, the legal structure, the roadmap, and how the marketing claims hold up.

A note on how this was written. Signature's own public commitment is that nothing gets described as built until it actually is: as its Trust page puts it, "if a control isn't real yet, it isn't listed here." This documentation holds itself to the same standard. Where something is still a work in progress, it says so plainly rather than smoothing it over.
