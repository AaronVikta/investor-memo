How you get in

You don't sign up.
You're brought in.

Every member arrives through someone who already trusts them, or through our own vetting. That's not a growth mechanic. It's the whole point of a cooperative: you know who you're in business with.

What you actually hold

A unit is a real claim,
not a promise on a screen.

The cooperative holds each asset in trust, for the members who capitalized it, and records your exact interest in it on paper you can read. Your name isn't on the title. Your ownership is still real, and it's recorded, not implied.

What we won't say

We won't tell you
what you'll earn.

No projections. No target returns. What we show you is a worked example: real arithmetic on a real distribution, run backward from what an asset actually earned, not forward from what we hope it will.

What's slower on purpose

You can't cash out
on a whim.

Your capital moves through scheduled windows, not whenever the mood strikes. A building doesn't sell in an afternoon, and pretending otherwise is how platforms quietly become unable to pay everyone at once. We'd rather be honest about the wait.

What can't be undone

Every entry stays.
Nobody edits history.

Every deposit, every distribution, every transfer is written once and locked. Not by policy. By the system itself. We can't quietly fix a number after the fact, and neither can anyone else.

This isn't for everyone.
It's for people who'd rather
own something real, slowly,
together.
